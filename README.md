# SafeRoute

### Bringing people who need to ship cargo together with available carriers.

A logistics app that connects people and businesses with cargo to ship with carriers ready to transport it.

## About SafeRoute

Finding a suitable transportation option means matching a shipping need with someone ready to carry the load. SafeRoute was created to bring both sides together in one app.

The app is for people and businesses that need to ship cargo, as well as carriers looking for transportation opportunities. It brings together topics related to cargo transportation operations and finances.

## Who it's for

- **Shippers:** people and businesses that need to transport cargo.
- **Carriers:** professionals and businesses available to provide transportation.

## What the app covers

- **Costs:** information related to transportation costs.
- **Trailers:** references to transportation vehicles and equipment.
- **Freight:** topics related to freight services.
- **Finances:** resources related to managing transportation finances.
